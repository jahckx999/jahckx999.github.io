About KAELENVASH

A universe of connected worlds

KAELENVASH is a Brazilian portal that brings together, in one place, free digital projects to expand consciousness, mind and prosperity. Each “world” has its own purpose, but they all share the same identity: clear, welcoming and accessible knowledge, guarded by the sacred geometry of our mandala.

Who is behind it

kaelenvash.com was created and is maintained by Jackson Davi Caldas Teixeira, founder of the KAELENVASH brand, passionate about nature, freedom and knowledge. The project combines human creativity with artificial intelligence tools to produce and maintain quality content, always with editorial review and responsibility.

Our principles

  • Original, free content, supported by discreet advertising.
  • Respect for traditions: spiritual themes are presented as the teachings of each tradition, without proselytism and without promises.
  • Accuracy: sources are cited, science is presented according to current consensus, and corrections are made whenever an error is pointed out.
  • No professional advice: nothing here replaces medical, psychological, legal or financial guidance.
  • Privacy: we ask for as little data as possible. Learn more in our Privacy Policy.

Advertising

To keep everything free, the site displays or may display Google AdSense ads, always labeled and kept separate from the content. Ads do not influence what we publish.
