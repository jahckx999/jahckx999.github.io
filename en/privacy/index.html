Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10/26/2026

This policy explains, simply and transparently, how kaelenvash.com handles the information of people who visit the site, in accordance with Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (LGPD — Law No. 13,709/2018) and with Google AdSense policies.

This is a translation of the original policy in Portuguese. In case of any discrepancy, the Portuguese version prevails.

1. Who is responsible

kaelenvash.com is maintained by Jackson Davi Caldas Teixeira (KAELENVASH brand), in Brazil, who acts as the controller of the personal data processed on this site. For any privacy matter, write to estudio@kaelenvash.com.

2. What this policy covers

It applies to the entire kaelenvash.com domain, including the showcase and the portals published on it, such as Teia Universal and Teia das Línguas. Features specific to each portal (for example, the Teia das Línguas study account) may have complementary rules described in that portal’s own policy. External sites we link to, such as social networks, follow their own providers’ policies.

3. What data we process

  • Browsing without registration: the showcase does not ask for your name, email or registration to be visited.
  • Technical data: as on any website, the hosting server (GitHub Pages) and third-party services may log data such as IP address, browser type, pages accessed and date and time, in order to deliver content and keep the site secure.
  • Fonts: the site’s fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which involves sending your IP address to Google.
  • Local storage: some portals store preferences and progress only in your browser (local storage) — for example, the language you chose — without sending them to us.
  • Voluntary contact: if you write to us by email, WhatsApp or social media, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, contact details and message.
  • Payments: services hired from Estúdio Kaelenvash are paid via Pix (Brazil’s instant payment system), directly in your bank’s app. We do not receive or store card details or banking passwords; a receipt only reaches us if you decide to send it.

4. Cookies and advertising (Google AdSense)

This site displays or may display ads provided by Google AdSense. It is important that you know:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendors’ cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices and youronlinechoices.eu.
  • To understand how Google uses data on partner sites, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where required by the law of the visitor’s region (such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), a notice will be shown to collect your consent before personalized advertising cookies are used. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; this does not prevent access to the content, but it may make ads less relevant.

5. Audience measurement

At the moment, the showcase does not use audience analytics tools. If we start using one (for example, Google Analytics), this policy will be updated beforehand, stating what data is collected and how to opt out.

6. Why we use data and on what legal basis

  • Delivering the site securely and preventing abuse — legitimate interest.
  • Answering messages and providing services you request — performance of a contract or of preliminary procedures at the data subject’s request.
  • Displaying ads that keep the content free — consent, where required, or legitimate interest, under the LGPD.
  • Complying with legal obligations — where applicable.

We do not sell personal data.

7. Sharing

Data may be processed by the providers that make the site possible: GitHub (hosting), Google (fonts and ads), Supabase (Teia das Línguas accounts) and the messaging platforms you choose to use. We may also share information if there is a legal obligation or an order from a competent authority. Some of these providers may process data outside Brazil, with the safeguards provided for in the LGPD.

8. How long we keep it

Messages and receipts are kept only for as long as needed to handle your request and comply with legal obligations. Data stored in your browser is under your control and can be deleted at any time in its settings.

9. Your rights

Under the LGPD (art. 18), you may request at any time: confirmation that we process your data; access; correction; anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data; portability; information about whom we share it with; and withdrawal of consent. Just write to estudio@kaelenvash.com. We will reply within a reasonable time, and, if you prefer, you may also contact Brazil’s National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) or the data protection authority of your country.

10. Security

The site uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and content security policies that limit what can be loaded on its pages. No system is 100% invulnerable, but we take reasonable measures to protect information.

11. Children and teenagers

The content is educational and open to everyone, but it is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you are a parent or guardian and believe this has happened, contact us so the data can be removed.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect new features or legal requirements. The date at the top shows the most recent version.

Questions? Talk to us through the Contact page or by email at estudio@kaelenvash.com.
